Which app is for what

All apps, logins, and common questions

ParentSquare

Run by OUSD and the school office

Official messages from the district, Principal Scott, and teachers. Attendance alerts and permission slips.

Created automatically from the contact info on file. Sign in with that email or mobile number.

Open ParentSquare

Konstella

Run by The PFC

Family directory, your child's classroom page, volunteer sign-ups, RSVPs, and the newsletter archive.

One parent per child gets an email invitation each August. Missing it? Email vpcommunications@thornhillschool.org.

Open Konstella

Aeries Parent Portal

Run by OUSD

Grades and the standards-based report card.

Needs your child's student ID and a verification code from the school office.
