Every way to help

An hour

  • Take a shift at the Fall Sweep or Spring Clean workday (Garden and Green Team).
  • Track waste at a school event (Garden and Green Team).
  • Give a book from the Library Wishlist.

An event

  • Help the BAC Events working group with the Fall Festival, Ruby Bridges Day, or ThornChill.
  • Help with the spring Read-a-thon (Library).
  • Join Thunder Dads for Kids Night Out and fix-it jobs.

A season

  • Take an open PFC board role: Auction Chair, Auditor, Grants and Solicitations, Site and Safety, Parliamentarian.
  • Serve on the School Site Council. Elections are in the fall.
  • Garden maintenance, an hour a week.

Most roles with students need the green clearance badge from Oakland EdFund: a TB test, fingerprints, and a signed waiver. Start now, since it can take a few weeks. How to get the badge
