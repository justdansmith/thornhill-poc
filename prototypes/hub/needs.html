Fill the gaps in fifteen minutes

One short conversation with each group's lead fills a row. These are the questions.

  1. What is the one thing your group has to get done this year?
  2. Where are you short: people, money, skills, or time with the school?
  3. What could a parent do for you in an hour, in one event, and over a season?
  4. Who leads now, and who could take over next year?
  5. Which dates should families have on their calendars?

Where this comes from

Known cells come from each group's page on thornhill.ousd.org, the PFC board roster, and the school calendar. Everything marked Ask the lead is still open.
