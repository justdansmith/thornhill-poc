Heritage project

A short presentation about your child's family: its culture, traditions, or history.

Pick one topic

  • A specific person, like a grandparent or another family member you interview
  • A family tradition, such as a holiday your family celebrates
  • Traditional foods or a special family recipe
  • A country of origin, your child's or a family member's
  • Family photos and the stories they tell
  • Family artifacts or heirlooms
  • A family tree or community map of the people central to your child's life

What to include

At least five details about the topic. A family tree means details about at least five family members; a country means at least five things about it, like foods, landmarks, or traditions.

Any format works

A diorama, Google Slides, a video, a book or cookbook, or an interview with a timeline of the person's life.

Due and presentations

  • Due the morning of Monday, November 30, the Monday after Thanksgiving break.
  • Presentations run Monday, November 30 to Friday, December 4, in the morning. Each child shares for one to two minutes, and families are welcome to come.
  • Your child's day and time are in Mr. J's project letter.
