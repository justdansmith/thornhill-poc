BAC Advisory Council

The Black, Indigenous, and People of Color Advisory Council is a parent-led group, formed in February 2023, that works so every student, especially those from underrepresented communities, feels included and belongs at Thornhill.

About

  • A subcommittee of the School Site Council that advises school leadership on policies and practices.
  • Works with the PFC on events and initiatives, from the Fall Festival and Ruby Bridges Day to ThornChill and the TK and kindergarten prospective family tours.

Ways to get involved

  • Parents and guardians of BIPOC students: join as a member and volunteer with a working group (Policies and Procedures, Events, or Communications).
  • Everyone else: become a BAC Ally. Attend meetings and support the BAC's work.
