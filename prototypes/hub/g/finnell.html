Costumes

  • No masks.
  • No costumes of someone's culture.
  • Nothing too gory or scary.
  • No weapons as accessories, such as swords or guns.
  • A child who can't follow the Safe, Mindful, Responsible expectations changes into spare clothes.

What Room 1 is learning

  • Letters Bb and Ee. The class now knows all five vowels that sit in the middle of CVC (consonant, vowel, consonant) words.
  • Lowercase e is tricky: early readers often read it as /i/ or as its name, and its pen path takes practice.
  • b and d reversals are normal at this age. Correct them at home too, since every wrong rep makes the wrong way stick.
  • 2D and 3D shapes: 2D shapes are flat, and 3D shapes take up space. Name everyday things by their shape, like a soup can (a cylinder) or a tissue box (a rectangular prism), and talk about sides, corners, and the math word vertices.
  • Observing weather patterns.

Check for lice

Lice is going around Thornhill, including Room 1. Check your child's hair often in direct sunlight, especially behind the ears and at the nape of the neck, and watch for itching and scratching. Ms. Finnell recommends an appointment at NitPixies, since home treatments seldom clear lice completely.
