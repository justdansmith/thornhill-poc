Garden and Green Team

The Green Team works on waste reduction and school-wide sorting, the garden learning program, and sustainability at school and PFC events. The garden gives students hands-on biology and ecology in raised beds, fruit trees, a native plant habitat, and a woodland garden.

This year's priorities

  • Waste reduction and sorting in classrooms, the cafeteria, and school events, with compostable and reusable products at events.
  • Healthy food options at every school-wide event.
  • School grounds: litter, landscaping, and a shared sense of care.

Where help is needed

  • New adult and student members.

Ways to get involved

  • Help cut single-use plastic in snacks and lunches.
  • Help with garden maintenance, as little as an hour a week.
  • Support student-led sustainability projects.
  • Track waste at a school event.
  • Take a shift at the Fall Sweep or Spring Clean workday.
