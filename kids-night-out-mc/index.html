The secret signal

Teach this before the rules. It's your most important tool all night.

Say

When I say Thunder, you say BOOM. And after BOOM, the room goes totally silent, like a storm that just stopped. Thunder!

Do

Kids yell BOOM. Freeze, finger to lips, wait for silence.

Say

Not bad. Now whisper it. Thunder. Now the loudest BOOM in the history of Thornhill. THUNDER!

The trick roundCatch them with fake Thunders
Say

Careful, I'm tricky. If I say anything other than Thunder, you stay quiet. Ready? Thunder-pants!

Do

Half the room yells BOOM. Laugh, then catch them once or twice more: Thunder-burger, Thunder-armpit, and finally a real Thunder.

Signal tips and backupsWait for silence; other call-and-responses
After you use it, wait in silence until the room is fully quiet. Never talk over them. Then thank the first group that froze: "This row froze in one second. Amazing."
Backup signals if Thunder wears out: "Macaroni and cheese!" / "Everybody freeze!" or, for October, "Hocus pocus!" / "Everybody focus!"

The Thunder Rules

About 3 minutes. Hold up a finger for each rule, and let kids guess each one before you say it.

Say

Rule one: we stay in the room. This is home base tonight. Need the bathroom? [bathroom rule]. Every single time.

Say

Rule two: walking feet. Running is for outside. In here, we walk, and we walk around sleeping bags, never over them. Even if you see pizza.

Say

Rule three: kind hands, kind words. Keep your hands and feet to yourself. And if you see somebody playing alone, invite them in.

Say

Rule four: when you hear Thunder, you BOOM and freeze.

Games to settle in

Play in the open half of the room, away from the sleeping bags. Little or no equipment, and nobody gets "out" for long.

Would You Rather, wall to wallAll ages · 5 to 10 min · no equipment
Say

I'll give you two choices. Pick one by walking (walking!) to that wall. Would you rather... have a pet dragon? That wall. Or a pet unicorn? That wall.

  1. Have a pet dragon or a pet unicorn
  2. Eat pizza for breakfast every day or ice cream for dinner every day
  3. Be able to fly or be invisible
  4. Have spaghetti for hair or sweat maple syrup
  5. Be a ghost or a vampire for Halloween
  6. Live in a treehouse or a submarine
  7. Talk to animals or speak every language
  8. Have T. rex arms or a giraffe neck
  9. Be as tiny as an ant or as big as a house
  10. Never have homework or never have a bedtime
Make it land. After each one, ask one kid from each wall "Why?" End with: "Last one. Would you rather sit down quietly right now, or sit down quietly right now?"
Rock Paper Scissors Cheerleader TournamentAll ages · 5 to 8 min · loud, and everyone stays in
  1. Everyone finds a partner and plays one round.
  2. The loser becomes the winner's cheerleader, follows them, and chants their name.
  3. Winners find another winner. The whole cheer squad follows.
  4. Final round: two kids face off with half the room cheering for each.
Say

If you lose, you're not out. You become the biggest fan of the person who beat you, and your job is to cheer their name as loud as you can.

Thunder SaysBest for TK to 2nd · 5 min
Say

It's like Simon Says, but Thunder is the boss. Thunder says touch your nose. Thunder says flap like a chicken. Jump! Gotcha!

Moves: freeze like a statue, zombie walk, T. rex roar, spooky ghost float, tiny mouse, giant bear. Caught kids do a silly dance and jump right back in.

Freeze DanceAll ages · 5 min · phone and speaker
Say

When the music plays, dance. When it stops, freeze like a statue. This round, dance like a robot.

Theme rounds: robot, ghost, spaghetti noodle, slow motion, your grown-up at a wedding. Kids who move during a freeze become judges.

Say

After the movie

8:15 to 9:00. Pack up, clean up, dance, then quiet time while families sign out. Each step has a slide.

Nothing flies, all night. Balloon keepy-uppy got little kids hurt: kids chasing something overhead don't see each other.

Limbo time! One line, no running starts. Lean back and go under without touching the stick. Every round it goes lower. How low can you go?

4. Quiet time while families sign out

Until 9:00, all kids out · slides: Quiet time, Quiet riddles, Silent Simon

Say

Everybody sit down in front of the stage. When a helper calls your name, grab your stuff and meet your grown-up at sign-out. Everyone else whisper-waves goodbye.

More pack-up ideasOther ways to call kids up, lost items
  • Other ways to call groups. By grade (TK and K first), by birthday month, or by the first letter of their name.
  • Lost Treasure sweep. Thunder Captains check every spot for socks, water bottles, and stuffed animals. Anything found goes in a Lost Treasure pile on the stage.
  • Burrito check. Before the parade, everyone sits on their burrito so you can spot the ones still unrolled.
More cleanup ideasRows, little helpers, beat the clock
  • One monster per row. Each row gets its own pizza box. The first row with a clean floor sits down and picks the first dance song.
  • Little helpers. TK and K kids are the Monster Feeders; big kids hold the boxes and do the chomping.
  • Beat the clock. When the timer ends, everyone freezes. Anything still on the floor gets a giant groan.
Songs that run the dance for youLine dances, littles, freeze
  • Cha Cha Slide and Cupid Shuffle call out the moves, so the whole room moves together.
  • Hokey Pokey for the littles.
  • Freeze Dance with any song. Press Space on the slide to flip DANCE and FREEZE.
  • Dance like a... The Mystery Trash finder picks the next move.
More quiet sign-out activitiesRiddles, Silent Simon, drawing, circle games
  • Quiet riddles. Eight on the slides. Kids whisper a guess, then click to show the answer.
  • Silent Simon. Do slow moves with no words, and the kids copy.
  • Draw Along. "Draw a big circle. Two pointy ears. Three eyes. A giant smile with one tooth. A bow tie. Name your monster!"
  • Monster Mash-Up. Fold paper in three. One kid draws a head, the next a body, the next the legs. Unfold and name it.
  • Telephone, Story Circle, What Changed? All work sitting in front of the stage, and kids can leave mid-game.

If it gets loud

Seven quick fixesNoise, running, bored big kids, little kids
  • Room too loud. Signal once, then wait in silence. Drop to a whisper; kids lean in to hear.
  • Praise what you want to see. "This row froze in one second" works faster than "Stop talking."
  • One kid won't listen. Quiet word, at eye level, away from the group. Offer two good choices.
  • Energy dropping. The Rock Paper Scissors tournament is the fastest restart.
  • Big kids bored. Make them Captains or judges.
  • Moving games with little kids. Split littles and bigs, or have the big kids play on their knees. Nothing that flies overhead.
  • Running. "Rewind!" Walk it back in slow motion.
