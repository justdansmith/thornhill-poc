From our October 13 call. Three things to try, smallest first, and the questions for us to decide.

For Jon, Selam, Annie, and Priya

1Point families to ParentSquare Digest

ParentSquare already lets each parent switch to Digest: one summary in the evening instead of a message for every post. Urgent alerts still arrive right away.

Most families don't know the setting exists.

Try: a two-line how-to in the next newsletter and on the website.

To decide

  1. Who sends the how-to, and when?
  2. Does Konstella have a similar setting? (Annie)

2Let families choose what they get

A short sign-up with three questions:

  • Which classes are yours?
  • How much do you want? Must-know only (no-school days, deadlines, pickup changes), plus PFC events and ways to help, or everything.
  • How often? One email on Sunday, as it happens, or urgent only.

The Sunday email is assembled from the website: the week's dates for their grades, their teacher's note, PFC events, and one way to help. About 21 versions by grade and depth, one review before it goes out.

Pilot: kindergarten families and the board for four weeks. We'd watch opens, unsubscribes, a one-tap "did you miss anything?", and repeat questions to the office.

To decide

  1. Should the Sunday email replace the Tuesday newsletter, or run beside it during the pilot?
  2. Who reviews and sends it each week?
  3. Which tool sends it: Konstella or a separate email tool?
  4. Which grade pilots first?

3Try an AI helper for parent questions

An "Ask Thornhill" box on the website. It answers only from the website, the school calendar, and the FAQ, names the page it used, and replies in English or Spanish. When it doesn't know, it says who to ask. Anything about a specific child (attendance, grades, health) goes to the office.

Estimated cost per answerabout $0.001 to $0.007
1,000 questions a monthabout $1 to $7
Hard monthly cap (it switches off and points to the office)$20
Most a three-month pilot can cost$60

Estimates from Anthropic's published prices for Claude Haiku 5.5, its smallest current model.

Guardrails: ten questions per visit, a daily limit per device, a bot check, and topics limited to Thornhill. The questions parents ask also show us what the website is missing.

To decide

  1. Is up to $20 a month a reasonable three-month experiment?
  2. Who pays: a PFC account and card?
  3. Do we need Principal Scott's OK before families see it?
