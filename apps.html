Which app do I use?

Thornhill families hear from the district, the school, and the PFC through a few different tools. Here is what each one is for and how to get in.

ToolUse it forHow to get inStuck?
ParentSquare
Run by OUSD (the district and the school office)		Official messages from the district, Principal Scott, and teachers. Attendance alerts, emergencies, district forms, permission slips, and direct messages to teachers.Your account is created automatically from the contact info the school has on file. Sign in at parentsquare.com/signin with that email or mobile number, or use the ParentSquare app.
Open ParentSquare		Ask the Thornhill office to check your contact info. Changes take effect the next day.
Konstella
Run by The PFC		Everything ParentSquare does not cover: the family directory, your child's classroom page, volunteer sign-ups, RSVPs, photos, and the newsletter archive.One parent per child gets an email invitation each August. Create a login, add your child, and join their classroom. Anyone else in the family can request to join.
Open Konstella		Email vpcommunications@thornhillschool.org for an invite.
The Tuesday newsletter
Run by The PFC		One email every Tuesday with the week's events, PFC news, and school updates. Links also go out on ParentSquare and Konstella.Nothing to log into. It arrives by email. Past issues live in Konstella under Files and Links, then Newsletters.
Open the Stay Connected page		To submit an item, email newsletter@thornhillschool.org by 8 PM Thursday.
Aeries Parent Portal
Run by OUSD		Grades and the standards-based report card.Go to oaklandusd.aeries.net/student. To create an account you need your child's student ID and a verification code from the school office.
Open Aeries Parent Portal		The Thornhill office can look up the student ID and code.
School calendar
Run by The PFC and the school		Every school and PFC event in one place. Subscribe once and it shows up in your own Google Calendar.No login. Open the calendar on the website and subscribe.
Open School calendar		Missing event? Email webmaster@thornhillschool.org.
Facebook group and Instagram
Run by Parents, and the PFC		Social only. Facebook connects past and present families. Instagram @thornhillpfc shares photos. Neither carries official information.Request to join the Thornhill Elementary School Families group on Facebook. Follow @thornhillpfc on Instagram.
Open Facebook group and Instagram		Anything official is on ParentSquare, Konstella, or the website.

Common questions

I just enrolled. What do I set up first?

Three things. Check that ParentSquare messages reach you (the school office can confirm your email and phone). Accept the Konstella invitation that arrives in August, or request one from vpcommunications@thornhillschool.org. Subscribe to the school calendar.

How do I report an absence?

Email attendance@thornhillschool.org (preferred) or call the office at (510) 879-2157 and leave a voicemail.

Where are past newsletters?

In Konstella: open Files and Links, then Newsletters.

How do I get something into the newsletter?

Email the article and any photo to newsletter@thornhillschool.org by 8 PM Thursday. It goes out Tuesday.

Which app has my child's grades?

The Aeries Parent Portal at oaklandusd.aeries.net/student. For elementary report cards, choose Grades, then Standards Based Report Card.

I never got a Konstella invite.

One parent per child receives the August invitation. Email vpcommunications@thornhillschool.org with your child's name, grade, and classroom and the PFC will send one.

Who do I ask about before and after school care?

JCC East Bay runs Kids' Club at Thornhill. Details and registration are on the Before and After School Care page.

I want to volunteer in a classroom or on a field trip. What do I need?

OUSD requires a green clearance badge from the Oakland Ed Fund (TB test, fingerprints, and a liability waiver) for any volunteering with kids in an unsupervised setting. Start on the Volunteer page.

What does the PFC actually pay for?

The library and librarian, music for every grade, PE, teacher aides, the art program, the garden, technology, and more. The PFC spends about $1,100 per child each year beyond what the state funds. The Annual Fund goal this year is $265,000.

Login details come from OUSD's ParentSquare and Parent Portal pages and the PFC's Stay Connected page. The school office at (510) 879-2157 is the fallback for anything account related.
