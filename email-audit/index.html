02

Where it doubles up

The newsletter goes out twice. It ran 38 times on Konstella and was reposted on ParentSquare in 30 of those weeks: 68 sends for 38 issues. Selam's explanation on October 13: Konstella has never reached every family, so the office reposts to reach the rest.
Konstella delivers many posts twice. 97 of 183 Konstella posts arrived when posted and again in the Saturday weekly summary.
Repeats under the same title. 54 posts repeated a title from the same route within three weeks. "New additional minimum days" went out 6 times on ParentSquare.
One event, several routes. Halloween plans came in 8 posts from 4 routes. Minimum-day and no-school changes took 20 posts.

04

When it lands

142 posts (20%) arrived between 6 PM and 7 AM, and 70 (10%) on a weekend. The district posts in the daytime: 4% of its posts land in the evening. PFC and school posts do so 27% and 28% of the time.

Konstella stacked up on 39 days, with up to 4 posts in one day (February 12, 2026).

05

The district's share

OUSD sent 190 posts through ParentSquare, 27% of everything. Its posts run long, with a median of 381 words against about 151 for the school, so the district wrote 47% of all the words a family received.

All routes together came to 211,000 words. At 200 to 250 words a minute, that is 14 to 18 hours of reading a year.

07

What we could change

Within the three areas Jon scoped (the newsletter, Konstella, and the website), plus one ask of the office:

  1. Newsletter: every two weeks, posted once on the website. Konstella and ParentSquare each carry a short pointer to it. Every two weeks takes 68 sends a year to about 34.
  2. Konstella: one schedule, one post a day. Annie holds the calendar (Selam's step one). Stacked days carried 49 posts beyond the first; those move to the next open day or into the newsletter.
  3. Konstella: tell families about the weekly summary. Each family picks instant posts or the Saturday summary, instead of getting both for 97 posts.
  4. Website: one page per date and event. Minimum days, no-school days, and events live on one calendar families subscribe to, and every post links there. This year minimum days took 20 posts, and Halloween plans came from 4 routes.
  5. Send before 6 PM. Evening drafts go out the next morning. About one PFC or school post in four lands after 6 PM today.
  6. Ask the office about the district. District posts are 47% of the reading. Ask Principal Scott what the school can do about district volume, and show families the ParentSquare Digest setting from the October 23 ideas.
Take it further: rerun this count every quarter with two or three volunteer families adding their inboxes, and report the four headline numbers at each board meeting.

08

How this was counted

  • One parent's Gmail, read only, on October 28, 2026: 1,330 threads from Konstella, ParentSquare, @ousd.org staff, JCC aftercare, and PFC fundraising tools.
  • A post is one announcement. ParentSquare daily digests and Konstella weekly summaries were opened and their posts counted once each; 188 posts reached the inbox only inside a digest.
  • Left out: 14 receipts, invoices, sign-up confirmations, and password resets; family and personal email; board-role notices.
  • Limits: one family, its classrooms, and its notification settings. WhatsApp groups, texts, paper flyers, and Aeries are not counted. Times are when a post was sent.
